How it works

From first call to finished room

Most of the time on a loft is the drawings and approvals, not the building. So that is where we start.

Week 0

Free site survey

We look at the roof and talk through what you want the space for.

8 to 12 weeks

Design and approval

Drawings, building regulations and, where needed, planning with Sutton or Epsom and Ewell council.

6 to 10 weeks

The build

Structure, dormers, windows, insulation, electrics, plumbing and the bathroom.

Finish

Handover

Checked with you room by room, and the site left clean.
